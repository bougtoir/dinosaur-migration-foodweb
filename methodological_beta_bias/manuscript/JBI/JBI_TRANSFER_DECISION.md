# GEB → JBI transfer decision

- Transfer used: NO (submission prepared as a standard new JBI submission).
- Reason: the GEB decision was a desk rejection without external review,
  so a formal Wiley transfer would carry no reviews or reviewer goodwill;
  a standard submission is operationally simpler and loses nothing. If
  Wiley offers a one-click transfer that preserves files and metadata,
  it remains acceptable, but no disadvantage is apparent in a fresh
  submission either.
- Metadata/reviews transferred: none. If the JBI submission form asks
  about previous editorial assessment, disclose accurately and briefly:
  "Following editorial assessment at the sister journal Global Ecology
  and Biogeography, Journal of Biogeography was specifically recommended
  as a potentially stronger fit for this manuscript." The GEB rejection
  is otherwise not mentioned in the manuscript or cover letter.
