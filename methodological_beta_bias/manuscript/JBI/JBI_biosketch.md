# Biosketch (JBI)

Tatsuki Onishi is a quantitative researcher working on methodological
problems in spatial and temporal inference. His work develops controlled
simulation frameworks and reproducible analytical pipelines for comparing
assemblage and occurrence data across disciplines, with an emphasis on
traceable, frozen-seed reproducibility and value-level auditability. The
present study applies that programme to biogeographic inference from
structurally heterogeneous occurrence data.
