# Cover letter — Journal of Biogeography

Dear Editors,

I submit “Assemblage structure distorts beta diversity and distance
decay” for consideration in Journal of Biogeography. Using controlled
simulations and deep-time empirical stress tests, I show that unequal
regional richness, dominance, taxonomic aggregation and temporal
integration can generate or exaggerate apparent spatial-turnover
differences and distort distance-decay slopes even when generative
spatial processes are identical. I provide a practical diagnostic
workflow for distinguishing structural artefacts from interpretable
biogeographic signal. These results are directly relevant to comparative
biogeography using heterogeneous occurrence and assemblage data.

Sincerely,
Tatsuki Onishi
