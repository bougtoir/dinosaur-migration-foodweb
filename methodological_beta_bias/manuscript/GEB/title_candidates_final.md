# Final title shortlist — GEB (8 candidates)

1. **Structural asymmetries distort guild-level beta-diversity
   contrasts** — RECOMMENDED. Neutral direction ('distort' covers
   create/erase/exaggerate), mechanism-level generality, no taxon or
   formation names, directly matches the main result.
2. Assemblage structure can create, erase and exaggerate spatial
   turnover signals — vivid but 'erase' implies a settled direction;
   retained as alternate.
3. Assemblage structure reshapes beta diversity and distance decay —
   foregrounds the decay result; slightly narrower.
4. Regional diversity and temporal aggregation bias spatial-turnover
   inference — names two of the mechanisms; accurate.
5. When observed beta diversity misrepresents ecological turnover —
   readable, conceptual; less specific.
6. Structural sources of guild beta-diversity contrasts: a
   simulation and fossil stress test — methodological positioning.
7. Structural asymmetries bias guild-level turnover inference in
   aggregated assemblage data — adds 'aggregated' qualifier.
8. Guild-level beta diversity is sensitive to assemblage structure —
   weakest; too declarative.

Selection rationale: title 1 maximises GEB fit and conceptual breadth
while avoiding overclaim; avoids 'erase' (the temporal-aggregation
direction is source-dependent) and all dinosaur/Morrison terms.
