## Abstract

Ecologists routinely compare beta diversity and distance decay among guilds, taxa, habitats or data sources and interpret the differences as evidence of dispersal limitation, connectivity, environmental filtering or habitat breadth. Such comparisons assume that the compared assemblages are structurally comparable, yet they often differ in regional pool size, dominance, taxonomic resolution, temporal aggregation and sampling. Each mechanism is individually known to affect diversity metrics; less is known about how large their separate and joint effects on guild-level contrasts can be. I used controlled one- and two-dimensional simulations with a fixed generative guild contrast to manipulate these properties singly and factorially, quantifying their effects on mean pairwise Jaccard dissimilarity and on distance-decay slopes. I then applied a sequential structural decomposition to a predator–herbivore turnover contrast in Late Jurassic Morrison Formation dinosaur assemblages, with the Nemegt Formation as a structural comparator. With no generative contrast, a four-taxon versus 26-taxon pool produced an apparent contrast of [[sim01_bias_p4|2]] (two-taxon pool: [[sim01_bias_p2|2]]), and pool asymmetry steepened the apparent distance-decay slope ([[sim13_2d_decayslope_small_p4]] versus [[sim13_2d_decayslope_comparison_p4]]) under identical spatial processes. Temporal aggregation was source-dependent: full pooling erased a structural contrast ([[sim04_db_width1]] to [[sim04_db_width8]]) but amplified imposed ecological contrasts [[sim21_weak_fold|1]]- to [[sim21_strong_fold|1]]-fold. Structural factors combined non-additively (combined bias [[sim06_bias_p4_pd0.9_k3|2]] versus [[sim06_additive_p4_pd0.9_k3|2]] expected from summed single-factor effects). In the Morrison Formation, a naive contrast of [[emp_db_naive]] (95% CI [[emp_db_naive.lo]] to [[emp_db_naive.hi]]) attenuated to [[emp_db_minus_allosaurus]] after exclusion of the dominant predator; Nemegt showed no comparable contrast ([[nem_db_full|+3]]; [[nem_db_full.lo]] to [[nem_db_full.hi|+3]]). These magnitudes are scenario-specific demonstrations of sufficiency, not universal constants. Structural comparability should be diagnosed, using pool-matched nulls and dominance, resolution and aggregation checks, before ecological meaning is assigned to beta-diversity or distance-decay contrasts.
