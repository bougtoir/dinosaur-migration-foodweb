## Results

### Pool-size imbalance alone creates strong apparent guild contrasts

With the generative guild contrast fixed at zero, reducing one guild's regional pool generated substantial apparent differentiation. At a predator pool of four taxa against 26 herbivore taxa, mean Δβ_obs was [[sim01_bias_p4]] (95% replicate interval [[sim01_bias_p4.lo]] to [[sim01_bias_p4.hi]]); at the most extreme tested pool of two taxa it was [[sim01_bias_p2]] ([[sim01_bias_p2.lo]] to [[sim01_bias_p2.hi]]) (Fig. 2a). Defining a false contrast operationally as an absolute Δβ_obs above 0.1 (a magnitude threshold on the replicate estimate, not a significance criterion), the false-contrast rate was [[fc_rate_pool2_thr0.10|pct0]]% at pools of both 2 and 4 taxa. All replicates were negative up to a pool of 12 taxa, and [[sim01_propneg_p20|pct1]]% remained negative at a pool of 20. Only at equal pools (26 versus 26) did Δβ_obs centre on zero ([[sim01_bias_p26|4]]; [[sim01_bias_p26.lo]] to [[sim01_bias_p26.hi|+3]]). The false-contrast rate depended on the threshold (Table 1): at a pool of two taxa it remained [[fc_rate_pool2_thr0.20|pct0]]% even at a threshold of 0.20, whereas at a pool of four taxa it was [[fc_rate_pool4_thr0.10|pct0]]% at 0.05 and 0.10 but [[fc_rate_pool4_thr0.20|pct0]]% at 0.20, and at pools of 12 or more it was [[fc_rate_pool12_thr0.05|pct0]]% at every threshold. The 0.1 criterion is therefore an operational threshold whose sensitivity is characterised, not a universal benchmark.

The two-dimensional replication reproduced the pattern: Δβ_obs was [[sim13_2d_bias_p4|2]] at a four-taxon pool and [[sim13_2d_bias_p2|2]] at a two-taxon pool (Fig. 3a), showing that the effect is not an artefact of one-dimensional range geometry. Pool asymmetry also changed the apparent spatial form of turnover. Under identical generative spatial processes, the fitted slope of pairwise Jaccard dissimilarity against geographic distance (positive values indicate dissimilarity increasing with distance, i.e. distance decay of similarity) for the four-taxon guild was [[sim13_2d_decayslope_small_p4]] per lattice unit, compared with [[sim13_2d_decayslope_comparison_p4]] for the 26-taxon comparison guild (Fig. 3b): the small-pool guild appeared to have much steeper distance decay, although both guilds were generated by the same spatial process.

### Dominance, taxonomic lumping, temporal aggregation and sampling

A single dominant predator lowered Δβ_obs monotonically as its prevalence increased (Fig. 2b): bias was approximately [[sim02_bias_pD0.5_allolike|2]] at p_D = 0.5, the cell closest to the observed occupancy of the dominant Morrison predator ([[emp_allo_occupancy|pct1]]% of collections), and [[sim02_bias_pD0.9|2]] at p_D = 0.9.

Collapsing k geographically partitioned species into one genus column produced B_lumping up to [[sim03_B_lumping_k6|+3]] at k = 6, shifting Δβ_obs from [[sim03_db_species_k6]] at species resolution to [[sim03_db_genus_k6]] after genus pooling (Fig. 4a). Taxonomic lumping thus fabricated apparent continuity in the lumped guild.

Temporal aggregation had a source-dependent effect rather than a uniform one (Fig. 4b). In the structural arm, where the finest-resolution contrast arises only from pool asymmetry, pooling eight slices into one bin moved Δβ_obs from [[sim04_db_width1]] to [[sim04_db_width8]]: the structurally generated contrast was erased. Under imposed ecological contrasts, pooling instead amplified the contrast. Finest-resolution contrasts of [[sim21_weak_thin|2]], [[sim21_moderate_thin|2]] and [[sim21_strong_thin|2]] became [[sim21_weak_pooled|3]], [[sim21_moderate_pooled|3]] and [[sim21_strong_pooled|3]] under full pooling (attenuation fractions [[sim21_weak_attenuation_fraction|2]], [[sim21_moderate_attenuation_fraction|2]] and [[sim21_strong_attenuation_fraction|2]], i.e. [[sim21_weak_fold|1]]- to [[sim21_strong_fold|1]]-fold amplification). Under union pooling, taxa that are already widespread approach full occupancy first, which drives the per-bin dissimilarity of the guild with broader ranges toward zero disproportionately. The direction of the distortion therefore depended on which feature differed between the guilds, not simply on the width of aggregation.

An eight-fold sampling asymmetry between environmental blocks produced comparatively small and stable bias under the parameter range examined (Table 2). This result is specific to the tested parameterisation and does not imply that sampling structure is generally unimportant.

### Structural factors interact non-additively

In the factorial design, structural biases combined non-additively (Fig. 5). For a four-taxon pool with p_D = 0.9 and k = 3, the combined bias was [[sim06_bias_p4_pd0.9_k3|2]], whereas the additive expectation was [[sim06_additive_p4_pd0.9_k3|2]]; the additive expectation is the pool-only bias (four-taxon pool, p_D = 0, k = 1) plus the dominance-only and lumping-only increments read from the 26-taxon row of the same grid (p_D = 0.9 and k = 3, respectively, each relative to p_D = 0, k = 1). This is a descriptive comparison of replicate means; no inferential interaction term was estimated. The false-difference rate (absolute Δβ_obs above 0.1 with Δβ_true = 0) reached [[sim06_false_difference_max|pct0]]% across the low-pool, high-dominance and high-lumping region. The largest bias, [[sim06_max_abs_bias|2]], occurred in the degenerate cells where k = 6 collapses the whole four-taxon guild into one column, so that its turnover is zero by construction and Δβ_obs equals the negative of the comparison guild's mean dissimilarity; the largest bias among non-degenerate cells was [[sim06_max_abs_bias_nondegenerate|2]]. Because these extreme-bias regions arise from combinations of factors, correcting any single factor in isolation did not remove the false difference.

### Morrison worked example: the naive guild contrast

On the primary specification (collection assemblages, genus level, Jaccard), Morrison predators were much more spatially homogeneous than herbivores: Δβ = [[emp_db_naive]] (bootstrap 95% CI [[emp_db_naive.lo]] to [[emp_db_naive.hi]]). Read naively, this contrast would suggest exceptional spatial continuity of the predator guild, which is precisely the class of signal that Simulations 1–3 show can arise structurally.

### Sequential structural decomposition

Applying the diagnostic sequence (Fig. 6): (1) the observed Δβ of [[emp_db_naive]] was close to, but marginally more negative than, the lower bound of the gamma-matched null interval ([[emp_gamma_null_interval.lo]] to [[emp_gamma_null_interval.hi]]); unequal pool sizes account for much of the magnitude but do not by themselves generate a contrast as extreme as the observed one. (2) The observed value was more negative than the frequency-matched null interval ([[emp_freq_null_interval.lo]] to [[emp_freq_null_interval.hi]]), so occupancy-frequency structure alone does not reproduce it. (3) As a dominance-sensitivity diagnostic (not as a preferred representation of the assemblage), excluding the dominant predator, *Allosaurus*, reduced the contrast to [[emp_db_minus_allosaurus]] ([[emp_db_minus_allosaurus.lo]] to [[emp_db_minus_allosaurus.hi|+3]]). (4) As a taxonomic-grain sensitivity analysis, at species resolution the contrast attenuated to [[emp_db_species]] ([[emp_db_species.lo]] to [[emp_db_species.hi]]), in the direction expected from Simulation 3. (5) After regressing geographic extent on stratigraphic duration and occurrence count, the *Allosaurus* extent residual was [[emp_allo_extent_resid_km|1]] km (z = [[emp_allo_extent_z|2]]); the genus is not geographically exceptional for its sampling footprint. (6) In the exploratory presence model, the unpenalised fit reached pseudo-R² = [[emp_presence_pseudoR2|2]] but did not fully converge (quasi-separation; state coefficients [[emp_presence_state_coef_min|1]] to [[emp_presence_state_coef_max|1]]); the L2-penalised refit converged with pseudo-R² = [[emp_presence_pseudoR2_penalized|2]] (C = 1), supporting the same qualitative conclusion that collection covariates carry substantial presence structure.

### Nemegt comparator

Under the same locked specification, the Nemegt Formation, where the herbivore pool ([[nem_sub_n_genera_herbivore|int]] genera) is smaller than the predator pool ([[nem_sub_n_genera_predator|int]] genera), yielded Δβ = [[nem_db_full|+3]] (95% CI [[nem_db_full.lo]] to [[nem_db_full.hi|+3]]) (Fig. 6). The Morrison direction is therefore not reproduced; Nemegt shows that the Morrison pattern is not a general property of predator guilds, without serving as a biological replication.

### Independent evidence

The bounded evidence audit identified nothing that contradicts the structural interpretation. Trackway evidence for large Morrison theropods is consistent with wide distribution but not diagnostic of movement (Foster, 2015); feeding-trace evidence indicates a broad prey spectrum including scavenging (Drumheller et al., 2020), which bears on dietary generalism rather than mobility; and no direct test of *Allosaurus* movement was identified.
